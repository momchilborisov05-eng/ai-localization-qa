# Northline style guide (sample)

## Voice
- Energetic, confident and friendly, like a rider talking to another rider. No hype words ("revolutionary", "ultimate").
- Short sentences. Product copy is scanned on a phone: lead with the benefit.
- Product and model names (Riptide, Drift Pro, Trailblazer, Shred 150, Swell Hunter, Lockdown) always stay in English.
- Established English action-sports jargon may stay in English when riders in the target market use it (camber, rocker, directional twin, concave, shortboard, leash).

## Bulgarian (bg)
- Address the reader informally with "ти" (Карай повече, Комбинирай го...), never "Вие".
- Decimal comma (30,9), thousands separated with a space (20 000).
- Units in Cyrillic: мм, см, кг, г, ч. Keep °C and %.
- Currency: amount first, then the symbol with a space: 100 €.
- Quotation marks: „...“.
- Snowboard bindings are "автомати"; a dropper post is "телескопична колона".

## German (de)
- Address the reader with "du" (lowercase), never "Sie".
- Decimal comma (30,9), thousands separated with a period (20.000).
- A space between number and unit, including % (30 %) and °C (12–16 °C).
- Currency: 100 € (amount first).
- Quotation marks: „...“.
- Use "Werktage" for business days, consistently.
